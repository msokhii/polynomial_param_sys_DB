Parametric linear systems — specimen plate

One small concrete instance of every family in the benchmark database, shown as A x = b over its named parameters. Each matches ParametricSystems:-Show(id, n) in the Maple module, so what you see here is what the generator emits — scale n up to reach benchmark size. Rational-entry families (A5, A8) are cleared per row before solving; B11 is singular by design (its kernel is the invariant).
